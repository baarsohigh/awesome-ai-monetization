# Methodology

## Source hierarchy

Use official pricing pages, billing documentation, product docs, and announcements first. Credible reporting and investor research may support context, never replace a readily available official price. Every source records its URL, type, access date, supported fields, and confidence.

## Facts versus analysis

`observed` includes published plan facts, prices, units, credits, availability, and sales motion. `analysis` contains value-metric interpretation, expansion vectors, archetypes, and commentary. Verified means direct authoritative evidence; high and medium labels describe explicit analytical confidence, not proof.

## Freshness

Fresh is 0–30 days since verification; Recent 31–90; Aging 91–180; Stale more than 180. A stale record is not assumed false—it is a visible research priority.

## Change workflow

Monitor only public, permitted URLs at a low rate. A content difference becomes a candidate, not an update. A maintainer verifies the affected normalized facts, adds a dated snapshot and pricing event, then publishes the change.
